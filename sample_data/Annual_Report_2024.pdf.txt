COAL INDIA LIMITED (A Maharatna Public Sector Undertaking)
50th ANNUAL REPORT & STATUTORY ACCOUNTS (FY 2023-24)

--- PAGE 1 ---
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:
Coal India Limited (CIL) continued its historic growth trajectory in FY 2023-24, achieving an all-time record production and dispatch. Through strategic mechanization, deployment of Continuous Miners, and high-capacity Surface Miners, operational efficiency was enhanced across all subsidiary command areas.
