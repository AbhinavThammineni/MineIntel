OPERATIONAL HEADWINDS IN BCCL COMMAND AREA:
Why did BCCL production decrease in 2023?
Production was adversely affected by prolonged equipment downtime, heavy monsoonal pit inundation, and geological faulting in Jharia coalfield. Essential spare parts for heavy electric shovels were delayed by supply chain bottlenecks.

--- PAGE 28 ---
GEOTECHNICAL AUDIT - ECL RAJMAHAL:
Rajmahal opencast output declined in 2023 due to severe slope instability, overburden backlog, and monsoonal flooding in the central pit. Safety mitigation measures mandated restricted operations along northern bench cuts.
